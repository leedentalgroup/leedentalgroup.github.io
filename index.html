Your safety first

Patient Screening & Safety

Our clinic follows the guidelines provided by the ADA, CDC and WHO to keep every visit safe. The health and safety of our staff and patients are our top priorities.

We thank you in advance for your cooperation, understanding and patience. Here are the guidelines we ask every patient to follow, and the changes we have made at our clinic:

Before your visit

  • Please bring your own pens to avoid unnecessary contact. 오실때 불필요한 접촉을 피하기 위하여 환자 본인의 필기도구를 가져오세요.
  • A mask is recommended for everyone's safety, especially if you have a cough or cold symptoms. 서로의 안전을 위하여, 특히 기침이나 감기 증상이 있으시면 마스크 착용을 권장합니다.
  • We limit access to the office to the patient only, whenever possible. 보호자가 필요한 환자를 제외하고는 환자 본인만 들어오시기 바랍니다.
  • We check body temperature before any procedure. 진료 시작전 체온 측정이 있습니다.
  • Please let us know if you develop any signs or symptoms of illness within 14 days of your visit. 진료후 14일 이내에 감염성 질환 증상이 있으신 경우 꼭 연락해 주십시요.

How we keep the clinic safe

  • With limited staff, please be patient with any waiting times.
  • Our clinic has installed glass panels around the reception area.
  • Our staff members are provided with extra protective gear.
  • Our clinic provides an abundance of hand sanitizer and performs thorough cleanings before each business day.
